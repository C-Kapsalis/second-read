# Social posts: hard checks

Name the platform first. If the user did not, infer it from the copy
(hashtag style, "link in bio", handles, length) and say which you assumed.
Count characters with spaces. Each failure needs a quote or a number.

## Platform limits

| Platform | Hard limit | Visible before "see more" | Hashtags | Links |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| X | 280 characters on a standard account; every link counts as 23 | The whole post | 1-2 | Clickable |
| LinkedIn | 3,000 characters | About the first 210 characters (less on mobile) | 3-5 | Clickable |
| Instagram | 2,200-character caption; 30 hashtags maximum | About the first 125 characters | Inside the 30 limit | Not clickable in a caption; use "link in bio" or a link sticker |
| Facebook | Very long posts allowed | About the first 125 characters on mobile | 0-3 | Clickable |

## Checks

| # | Check | Fails when | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Hard limit | Over the platform's hard limit (X over 280 with links counted as 23; Instagram over 2,200 characters or over 30 hashtags). The post cannot go out as written | Blocker |
| S2 | Hook position | The news, benefit or offer is not inside the visible part (column 3). Pleasantries, weather, "Hope you're all well" or backstory fill the cut | Major |
| S3 | Hashtag count | Above the platform norm (X more than 2, LinkedIn more than 5, Facebook more than 3) | Minor (Major when they push the point below the cut) |
| S4 | Link placement | A URL in an Instagram caption (not clickable); a shortened or http:// link | Major |
| S5 | Paid partnership | Context says the post is paid, gifted or affiliate, and there is no clear disclosure ("Ad", "Paid partnership" label) before the cut. #ad buried at the end among other hashtags does not count | Blocker |
| S6 | Giveaway rules | A giveaway without: how to enter, closing date and time with time zone, who can enter (age, location), how the winner is picked and told, and, on Instagram or Facebook, a line that the platform does not sponsor or run it | Blocker |
| S7 | Engagement bait | "Comment YES", "Tag 3 friends", "Share if you agree", "Like if": platforms demote it | Major |
| S8 | One action | More than one way to act competing (call, DM, app, link, visit) with no primary; vague CTA ("Learn more") | Major |
| S9 | Offer facts | A price, discount, code or deadline missing, vague ("limited time") or wrong (check the arithmetic) | Major. Blocker when the arithmetic is wrong |
| S10 | Health, money, safety claims | Results promised ("cleared my eczema", "guaranteed 8% returns", "zero risk") | Blocker |
| S11 | Unsourced numbers | A statistic with no source, sample or date ("87% of CFOs say...") | Major |
| S12 | Brand voice | A line contradicts the brand's stated voice or the rest of the post (insults, pressure, a tone switch) | Major |
| S13 | Accessibility | An image described with no alt text; text only in the image; emoji used as bullets for every line | Minor |

## Write for the share

A share puts the post in front of someone else's audience, so it is a much
stronger signal than a like. Copy that is useful, specific or surprising
earns shares; bait does not. Platform-native beats copy-paste: the same text
on X, LinkedIn and Instagram fails at least one of them.

## Rewrite rules for social

- Hook first: the point inside the visible part, ideally the first line.
- Stay under the hard limit and show the count ("262/280").
- Hashtags inside the norm, at the end, relevant to the post.
- One action. On Instagram, "link in bio" instead of a URL.
- Disclosure first for paid posts: "Ad" or "Paid partnership with [brand]" in
  the opening line.

## Usually fine in social (do not flag)

Emojis in casual B2C or creator voice; first-person voice from a named
person; a long LinkedIn post inside 3,000 characters; a complete code with
amount and expiry; required risk or regulatory lines; a sourced rating with a
date; numbered entry steps in a giveaway; a link in a LinkedIn, X or
Facebook post.

## You cannot check from pasted copy

The image or video itself, the link destination, reach and engagement,
whether the platform's paid-partnership label is switched on. Name them
under "Not checked".
