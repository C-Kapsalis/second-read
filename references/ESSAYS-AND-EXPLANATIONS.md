# Expert writing, explanation and nonfiction essays

Start with what this audience already knows. Trace concept introduction, major claim, supporting reasoning, examples, exceptions and conclusion in reading order. Highlight repeated premises consistently. Prefer a specific bridge or a moved explanatory example to simply demanding more detail. Separate evidence from illustration and analogy; a memorable example need not be statistically representative. Protect useful voice and rhythm, including occasionally long sentences. Proposed edits must improve intelligibility without turning the author's original expertise into bland generic prose.

Sequence question: “What can a first-time reader now use to understand the conclusion in the next paragraph?” If missing, propose the sentence or reordering that provides it.
