# Brand voice, short posts, emails and announcements

Use the original Second Read copy checks for factual consistency, offers, platform constraints and disclosures when applicable. But a short text can be semantically dense: analyze its implied promise, contextual dependencies, first impression, tone and likely misunderstandings. Never equate length with depth. Protect distinctive language, jokes, productive ambiguity and concise memorable metaphors. Do not force an explicit CTA or feature list when the author intends conversation or identity signaling.

Sequence question: “What would a reader seeing only this sentence and the stated context believe was promised?” Where that differs from the author's intent, propose one exact alternative sentence and explain what nuance changes.
